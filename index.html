DEEPSEEK: THE AI THAT SHOOK 2025

A new AI from China became one of the most searched things on earth.

Daily article — November 5, 2026  |  Silent Security Vault — Domenick Arlon Hall, Mobile, AL

DeepSeek is an AI chatbot, like Gemini and ChatGPT, that answers questions in plain words. Early in 2025 it took the world by surprise — it was fast, it was free to try, and suddenly everyone was searching for it.

What you need to know

  • It ranked among the top trending searches in the world in 2025. Google's Year in Search put DeepSeek seventh among the year's trending searches globally.
  • It does the same job as other AI chatbots. Ask a question, get an answer, ask it to explain again if the first answer is too hard.
  • New does not mean safer. Every AI tool needs the same rules: no passwords, no bank details, no private photos pasted in.
  • Check it like any other answer. If it tells you a fact, a price, or a setting to change, check it with a second source before you act.
  • The lesson is bigger than one app. AI moved from a tech story to a daily tool in 2025. Learning how to use one teaches you how to use them all.

What to do next

If you tried DeepSeek, tell me what you asked it. We will check the answer together and learn what to trust.

Source: Trend source: Google Year in Search 2025, released December 4, 2025, reported by TechCrunch, December 4, 2025, and Marketing Charts, December 22, 2025 — DeepSeek ranked seventh among the year's top trending searches globally, and appeared on both the global and U.S. top 10 lists.
